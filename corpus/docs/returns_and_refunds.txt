Harbourline Outfitters: Returns and Refunds Policy
Version 4.2, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Customer Operations.

1. Purpose
This policy explains when a customer can return an item bought from Harbourline Outfitters, how refunds are paid, and what staff must check before they approve a return. It applies to purchases made in our shops and on the website. Faulty products are covered by the Warranty Policy, not by this one.

2. The return window
Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. For shop purchases the 30 days run from the date on the receipt. Clothing that has been worn outdoors counts as used and cannot be returned for a change of mind.
A tent that has been pitched once on a lawn for a fit check is accepted back. A tent with peg marks, mud, or a damp smell is treated as used and is refused.
Items bought during the Winter Sale, which runs from 1 December to 15 January, get a longer window: they can be returned until 31 January.
Gifts can be returned within 60 days if the person returning them has the gift receipt. Gift returns are always refunded as store credit.

3. How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds are issued within 5 working days of the returned item being inspected at the warehouse. PayPal refunds are issued within 2 working days of inspection.
Shop purchases paid in cash are refunded in cash up to a value of 50 pounds. Above 50 pounds the refund is made by bank transfer within 7 working days.
A customer may choose store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method. Store credit is topped up by 10 percent of the refund value, and it does not expire.

4. Items that cannot be returned
The following can never be returned unless they are faulty:
- Fuel canisters and any other pressurised gas product.
- Embroidered or personalised items.
- Underwear, socks and swimwear once the hygiene seal has been broken.
- Opened sleeping bag liners.
- Gift cards.

5. Return shipping
Returns of faulty items are free. For a change of mind the customer pays a 4.95 pound return label, which they can print from the returns portal. If the customer is exchanging an item for a different size of the same product, the label is free.
Parcels heavier than 20 kg, such as family tents, are collected by PeakFreight. The collection fee is 12 pounds.

6. Items damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours and attach photographs. A replacement is shipped within 2 working days of the report being accepted. The customer does not need to send the damaged item back unless we ask for it.

7. Who can approve a refund
Shop staff can approve refunds up to 150 pounds. A refund from 150 pounds up to 500 pounds needs a duty manager. Anything above 500 pounds needs the regional manager and a second signature on the refund form.
A refund without a receipt is only ever given as store credit. It requires a photo ID check, and a customer is limited to 2 receipt-free returns in each calendar year.

8. Spotting abuse
If one account makes more than 3 returns in any period of 60 days, the account is flagged automatically and Loss Prevention reviews the pattern before any further refund is paid. Staff must not tell the customer that their account has been flagged.

9. Complaints about a refund
If a customer is unhappy with the outcome of a return, follow the escalation steps in the Customer Support Handbook. Do not promise a different outcome on the shop floor.
