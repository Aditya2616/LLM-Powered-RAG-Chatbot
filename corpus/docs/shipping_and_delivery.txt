Harbourline Outfitters: Shipping and Delivery
Version 2.7, effective 15 February 2026. Owner: Logistics.

1. UK delivery options
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs 3.95 pounds. It is free on orders of 60 pounds or more.
Next-day delivery costs 6.95 pounds. The customer must place the order before 2 pm for dispatch the same day.
Saturday delivery can be added to next-day delivery for an extra 4 pounds.
Click and collect is free. An in-stock item is ready for collection in the chosen shop within 2 hours of the order being placed during opening hours.

2. Heavy items
Items over 20 kg, such as family tents, are carried by PeakFreight instead of the normal parcel carrier. PeakFreight delivers inside a two-hour window, which the customer can choose for a fee of 9 pounds. Without the fee, the delivery is made at some point during the day.

3. The Scottish Highlands and the islands
Delivery to the Scottish Highlands and to islands adds a surcharge of 5.50 pounds and 2 working days to the usual delivery time. This applies to every delivery option except click and collect.

4. Ireland and the EU
Orders to Ireland and EU countries take 6 to 9 working days and cost 11.50 pounds. Import duty and VAT are charged by the destination country and are the responsibility of the customer. We do not collect them at checkout.

5. Things we cannot ship
We do not deliver to PO boxes. Fuel canisters are classed as dangerous goods. They can be sent by ground delivery within the UK only, and never to Ireland or the EU.

6. Dispatch and tracking
Items that are in stock are dispatched within 1 working day. The customer gets a tracking email as soon as the parcel is handed to the carrier. Parcels are carried by Northline Couriers. The tracking link works on the carrier's site and in the order history on the website.

7. Missing parcels
If tracking shows no movement for 7 working days, or the parcel is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, Logistics opens an investigation with the carrier. The customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund once the investigation has started. Staff should not wait for the investigation to finish before offering that choice.

8. Failed deliveries
Northline Couriers makes up to 2 delivery attempts and then holds the parcel at a local depot for 5 days. After that the parcel is returned to our warehouse and the customer is refunded the item price but not the delivery charge.

9. Delivery address changes
An address can be changed free of charge until the order has been dispatched. After dispatch the customer must contact the carrier directly. We cannot redirect a parcel once it has left the warehouse.
