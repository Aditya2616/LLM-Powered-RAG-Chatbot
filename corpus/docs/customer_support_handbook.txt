Harbourline Outfitters: Customer Support Handbook
Version 4.0, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Customer Operations.

1. Channels and opening times
Phone support is open from 9:00 to 18:00 Monday to Saturday and from 10:00 to 16:00 on Sunday. Live chat runs from 8:00 to 22:00 every day. Email is answered during the phone hours.

2. Response targets
- Phone: answer within 30 seconds.
- Live chat: first reply within 60 seconds.
- Email: first reply within 1 working day.
- Complaints: acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 5 working days.
- Complaints that were escalated to Tier 2: resolve within 10 working days.

3. How we talk to customers
Use the customer's name. Apologise once, sincerely, and then move on to fixing the problem. Never tell a customer to calm down. Do not blame the carrier, the warehouse or another team in front of the customer. If you do not know the answer, say so and promise a time when you will come back with it.

4. Support tiers
Tier 1 advisors handle first contact. Tier 2 senior advisors handle complaints that Tier 1 could not resolve, and anything involving a refund above 150 pounds. Tier 3 is the Customer Operations Manager, who decides on exceptions to policy.

5. Goodwill gestures
When we have let a customer down, an advisor can offer a voucher worth up to 20 pounds. A team leader can approve up to 50 pounds. Anything higher is decided by the Customer Operations Manager. Always record the reason for a gesture in the case.

6. Logging complaints
Every complaint is logged in CaseDesk with one category code: D for delivery, P for product, R for refund, or S for staff conduct. A complaint without a category code cannot be closed. If a case touches two topics, choose the one that the customer is most upset about.

7. Final responses
A written final response must be sent within 8 weeks of the complaint being received. If the customer is still unhappy at that point, the letter must explain that they can take the matter to an independent ombudsman.

8. Vulnerable customers
If a customer tells you they are in difficulty, for example because of illness, bereavement or a language barrier, add the vulnerable-customer flag to their account. Do not read from a script in these calls. Offer a callback at a time that suits them and keep the same advisor on the case wherever possible.

9. Call recording
Calls are recorded for training and dispute resolution. Recordings are kept for 90 days. A customer can ask to have a recording played back through a formal request to Customer Operations.

10. Quality checks
Team leaders score 5 calls per advisor each month against the quality form. The pass mark is 85 percent. An advisor who falls below the pass mark in two consecutive months gets a coaching plan, not a warning.

11. Peak periods
During Black Friday week, annual leave is not approved for support staff, and every team leader takes calls for at least two hours a day.
