Harbourline Outfitters: New Starter Onboarding Handbook
Version 5.0, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Team.

1. Welcome
This handbook describes what happens between signing your contract and the end of your probation. Questions go to the People Team lead, Priya Nair, or to your line manager.

2. Before your first day
Once your contract is signed we ask you to bring proof of your right to work to the People Team. Your till badge, uniform and, for office roles, your laptop are ordered at this point. You will receive a message naming your buddy, who is an experienced colleague who answers everyday questions during your first weeks.

3. Your first week
Follow these steps in order:
1. Arrive at 9:00 on your first day and collect your ID badge from reception.
2. Attend the safety induction, which takes 45 minutes and covers fire exits and manual handling.
3. Set up your single sign-on account and turn on multi-factor authentication.
4. Read the three core policies: Returns and Refunds, Warranty, and Customer Data.
5. Shadow an experienced colleague for three shifts.
6. Start the Level 1 product training, which includes pitching a tent and lighting a stove.
Your line manager signs off each step on your onboarding checklist.

4. Product training
The Level 1 product certificate must be completed within 6 weeks of your start date. It has a practical test. If you do not pass at the first attempt you can retake it once.

5. Uniform
You are issued two fleeces. The first is free. Further uniform items are taken from your pay in three equal monthly instalments unless your manager agrees otherwise. Name badges must be worn on the shop floor at all times.

6. Staff discount
During probation the staff discount is 30 percent. After probation it rises to 40 percent on own-brand products and 25 percent on products from other brands. The discount can be used by you and one nominated household member.

7. Probation and check-ins
Probation lasts 3 months. Your line manager holds three check-in meetings: one at 30 days, one at 60 days, and a final review at 90 days. Notes from each meeting are written down and you receive a copy. During probation either side can end the employment with one week of notice.

8. Pay
Salaries are paid on the last working Friday of each month. The first payment includes any days worked in the previous month. If your payslip looks wrong, tell the People Team within 5 working days so that it can be fixed in the next pay run.

9. Training budget
After probation every employee can use up to 300 pounds a year on external training, such as first aid or navigation courses, with the line manager's approval.

10. Who to ask
Pay and contracts: People Team. Equipment and accounts: the IT desk. Shift swaps: your shop manager. Anything that worries you: your buddy or your line manager, in confidence.
