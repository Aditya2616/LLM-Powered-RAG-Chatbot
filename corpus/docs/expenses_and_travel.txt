Harbourline Outfitters: Expenses and Travel Policy
Version 2.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

1. Principles
Spend company money as carefully as you would your own. Claim only what you spent on company business, and keep every receipt.

2. Making a claim
Submit expense claims in the Expenses app within 30 days of the date you spent the money. Claims older than that are only paid with the finance director's approval. A receipt is required for every item over 10 pounds. Once your claim is approved, the money is paid within 10 working days.

3. Who approves what
Your line manager approves claims up to 500 pounds. A head of department approves claims between 500 pounds and 2,500 pounds. The finance director approves anything above 2,500 pounds. You can never approve your own claim.

4. Travel by train and air
Book standard class rail travel and choose an advance fare at least 14 days before you travel whenever you can. Flights are only allowed when the equivalent train journey would take longer than 6 hours. Book economy class.

5. Travelling by car
If you use your own car for work, mileage is paid at 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25 pence per mile after that. Parking and congestion charges are refunded with a receipt. Fines are never refunded.

6. Hotels
The maximum room rate is 110 pounds per night, or 150 pounds per night in London. Breakfast is included in these limits if it is part of the room rate. Ask Finance before booking anything above the cap.

7. Meals
When you are away overnight you can claim up to 35 pounds a day for meals: 8 pounds for breakfast, 10 pounds for lunch and 17 pounds for dinner. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

8. Client hospitality
Meals with clients are limited to 40 pounds per head and need a director's approval before the event.

9. Company cards
Company cards have a limit of 1,000 pounds. If a card is lost or stolen, tell Finance straight away and report it to the bank within 24 hours. Card statements must be matched to receipts by the 5th of the following month.

10. Home working equipment
Employees who work from home can claim a one-off allowance of 250 pounds for a chair, desk or monitor. Keep the receipt. The equipment belongs to the company and must be returned when you leave.

11. Foreign trips
For trips outside the UK, ask Finance for the daily allowance rate for the country. Exchange rate differences are not reimbursed.
