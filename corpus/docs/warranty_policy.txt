Harbourline Outfitters: Product Warranty Policy
Version 3.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Product Quality.

1. What the warranty is
Every Harbourline own-brand product comes with a warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not replace the customer's statutory rights, and it is separate from the change-of-mind returns described in the Returns and Refunds Policy.
Products from other brands carry the maker's own warranty. Shop staff should direct customers to the manufacturer's claim process and must not promise a Harbourline repair for them.

2. Warranty periods
The length of the warranty depends on the product:
- Tents: 3 years.
- Down jackets: 5 years.
- Waterproof shell jackets: 2 years.
- Sleeping bags: 2 years.
- Rucksacks: 5 years on seams and zips.
- Stoves: 2 years.
- Head torches: 1 year.
The period starts on the date of purchase and is shown on the receipt.

3. Extending a sleeping bag warranty
A customer who registers a sleeping bag on the website within 28 days of buying it receives one extra year of warranty, which makes it 3 years in total. Registration is free and needs the order number.

4. What is covered
The warranty covers faults caused by how the product was made. Examples are seams that come apart, delamination of a waterproof membrane, zips that fail while being used normally, and baffles in a down jacket that split open.

5. What is not covered
We do not cover wear and tear from ordinary use, damage from sunlight over a long time, damage from misuse, repairs or changes made by anyone else, or damage caused by washing the product incorrectly. A common example of incorrect washing is using fabric softener on a waterproof jacket, which clogs the membrane.

6. Making a claim
1. The customer opens a claim on the warranty portal and enters the order number.
2. They upload at least three photographs showing the fault.
3. The Repair Centre in Sheffield assesses the claim and replies within 10 working days.
4. If the claim is accepted, the customer receives a prepaid label to send the product in.
Shop staff can start a claim on the customer's behalf at the till, using the same steps.

7. Outcomes
When a claim is accepted we choose between three outcomes: a repair, a replacement, or a credit. A credit is only offered if the product is no longer made and no equivalent exists. The credit equals the purchase price reduced by 20 percent for every full year since the purchase.

8. Repair times
A repair normally takes 15 working days from the day the Repair Centre receives the product. Between May and August the Repair Centre is busier and a repair can take up to 25 working days. Customers should be told this when they make a claim in the summer.

9. Passing the warranty on
The warranty can be transferred once, to a second owner, if that person has the original receipt. After one transfer the warranty cannot be moved again.

10. Contact
Warranty questions that staff cannot answer go to the Product Quality team at warranty@harbourline.example.
