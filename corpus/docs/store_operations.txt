Harbourline Outfitters: Shop Operations Manual
Version 3.3, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Retail Operations.

1. Opening hours
Shops are open from 9:00 to 18:00 on Monday to Saturday and from 10:00 to 16:00 on Sunday. From September to December the shops stay open until 20:00 on Thursdays. Shops are closed on Christmas Day, and on Boxing Day they open from 10:00 to 16:00.

2. Opening procedure
Two people must open the shop together. Turn off the alarm within 60 seconds of entering. Count the till float before the first sale. The float is 200 pounds on a normal day and 300 pounds on Saturdays. Check the fitting rooms and the fire exits before the doors open.

3. Cash handling
When a till holds more than 1,000 pounds, a manager takes the excess to the safe and records it on the safe-drop log. Counts are made by two people. If a till is out by more than 5 pounds, report the difference to the area manager the same day. Do not make up a shortfall from your own pocket.

4. Closing
Nobody closes the shop alone. At least two people stay until the doors are locked, the alarm is set and the takings are in the safe. Lone working after 17:00 is not allowed in any shop.

5. Stock counts
There is a full stocktake twice a year, in January and in July. Every Tuesday one product category is cycle counted, and differences are entered into the stock system by Wednesday lunchtime.

6. Footwear fitting
Staff must measure both feet, because most people have one foot larger than the other. Fit boots in the afternoon, when feet are slightly swollen, and ask the customer to try them with the socks they plan to walk in. Let them walk on the fitting ramp for at least a minute.

7. Receiving deliveries
Check every delivery against the delivery note before you sign. Report missing or damaged items to the warehouse within 24 hours. Take a photograph of any damaged box before opening it.

8. Manual handling
Nobody should lift more than 20 kg alone. Use the trolley for tents and ask a colleague to help with heavy boxes. Do not carry boxes up the stairs without a second person.

9. Fire safety
The fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 10:15. If the alarm sounds at any other time, leave through the nearest exit and gather at the assembly point marked on the fire plan. Shop managers keep the signing-in sheet and take a head count.

10. Breaks
Staff working a shift of more than 6 hours receive a 20 minute paid break. A lunch break of 45 minutes is unpaid. Breaks must be taken away from the shop floor.

11. Theft
Never chase or confront someone you think has stolen something. Press the silent alert button and give a description to the manager. The manager decides whether to call the police.
