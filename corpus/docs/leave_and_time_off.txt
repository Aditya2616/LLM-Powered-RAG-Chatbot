Harbourline Outfitters: Leave and Time Off Policy
Version 3.4, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Team.

1. Annual leave
A full-time employee receives 28 days of paid annual leave per year, which includes bank holidays. Employees gain one extra day for each full year of service, up to a maximum of 33 days. Part-time staff receive the same entitlement pro rata.
The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

2. Carrying leave over
Up to 5 days of unused leave can be carried into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be taken by 30 June, after which they are lost.

3. Booking leave
Request leave through the HR portal. For a request longer than 5 consecutive days, ask at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter requests need 3 working days of notice. Your line manager replies within 2 working days.
Shop staff cannot book leave between 1 and 24 December. At other times a maximum of 2 people from the same shop can be on leave on the same day.

4. Sickness
If you are ill, phone your line manager before 8:30 am on the first day. A text message or an email is not enough. You can self-certify for the first 7 calendar days of an illness. From the eighth day a fit note from a doctor is needed.
After 6 months of service you receive full pay for up to 10 days of sickness in a leave year. After those 10 days the company pays statutory sick pay only.

5. Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks. The first 18 weeks are paid at full salary, and the remaining 8 weeks are paid at the statutory rate. Paternity leave is 2 weeks at full pay, and can be taken at any time in the first 12 weeks after the birth. Adoption leave follows the maternity rules.

6. Compassionate leave
After the death of a spouse, partner, child or parent you receive 5 days of paid leave. For other close relatives, such as a grandparent or sibling, it is 2 days. Further unpaid time can be agreed with your line manager.

7. Volunteering day
Every employee can take one paid day each leave year to volunteer for a registered charity. The day does not count against annual leave. Tell your line manager which charity you will help.

8. Study leave
If you are taking a professional exam you can take up to 3 days of paid study leave per leave year. Evidence of the exam booking is needed.

9. Jury service
Time spent on jury service is paid at full salary for up to 10 days. After that you can claim loss of earnings from the court.

10. Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of more than one week must be approved by a director. It is only granted in exceptional circumstances, such as an extended trip abroad to care for a relative.
